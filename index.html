Trending Scam Radar

Real scams reported this week. Learn the script so you never fall victim.

HIGH VOLUME

⚡ Electricity Power Cut Notice

"Dear Consumer, electricity power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM tonight due to unpaid previous bill. Call officer immediately."

The Trap: They ask you to download a remote-access app (AnyDesk/TeamViewer) or pay ₹10 via a fake link to steal your bank details.
ScamShield Tip: Electricity boards never send SMS from 10-digit personal mobile numbers!
CRITICAL ALERT

👮 "Digital Arrest" & Fake Law Enforcement

Scammers call pretending to be Police/CBI/Narcotics Bureau claiming your Aadhaar or mobile was found linked to illegal contraband or money laundering.

The Trap: They keep you on Skype/video call for hours, showing fake badges and demanding "security deposit" transfers.
ScamShield Tip: Real police or court never conduct arrests or investigations via Skype or WhatsApp video!
RISING THREAT

💼 Part-Time Job / Telegram Task Scam

"Earn ₹3000-₹8000 daily by simply reviewing Google Maps locations or liking YouTube videos from home."

The Trap: They pay ₹150 first to build fake trust, then ask you to invest in "prepaid crypto tasks" where your funds get locked forever.
ScamShield Tip: Any job that requires YOU to pay money to get your earnings is 100% a fraud.
